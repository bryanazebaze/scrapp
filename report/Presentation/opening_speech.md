## 🎯 The Purpose of the Opening Speech
Before you show the jury technical diagrams, databases, or algorithms, you need to **hook their attention** and **make them feel the problem**. The opening speech is delivered while **Slide 01 (Title Page)** is on the screen, just after the official greetings and before you transition to the plan and introduction.

Here are three distinct, high-impact options for your opening speech. Choose the one that best fits your personal speaking style.

---

### Option 1: The Narrative Hook ("The House Hunt Story")
*Best for: Creating immediate emotional connection and relatability. Every jury member has either searched for a house or knows someone who has.*

> **[Slide 01 — Title Page]**
>
> "Distinguished members of the jury, dear guests, good morning. 
> 
> Before we dive into the code, the database schemas, or the system architecture of our project, I would like to start with a very common, everyday scenario. 
> 
> Imagine you are looking to rent a new apartment in Yaoundé or Douala. You open your phone. You search through Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats, Kasastay, Mapiole, and various other websites. After hours of scrolling, you find what looks like the perfect 2-bedroom apartment. On one portal, it is listed at 150,000 CFA. On another, the exact same apartment is listed at 180,000 CFA. And when you finally call the agent, they tell you it is actually 200,000 CFA—and that it was already rented out two weeks ago.
> 
> This is not a fictional story. This is the daily, frustrating reality of millions of Cameroonians trying to navigate a real estate market characterized by extreme fragmentation, lack of transparency, and pricing confusion. 
> 
> This frustration is the exact spark that created our project: **CentralImmo**—a smart real-estate data aggregator tailored for the Cameroonian market. 
> 
> On behalf of my teammates—Azebaze Bahehebeg, Mangouo Jacky, and myself, Azefack Kelcy—welcome to our defense. I will act as the thread of this presentation, speaking in English, while my teammates will dive into the technical details in French. Let us begin."

---

### Option 2: The Macroeconomic Hook ("Digital Growth vs. Information Chaos")
*Best for: A highly academic, structured, and professional tone that highlights the contrast between technology adoption and market inefficiency.*

> **[Slide 01 — Title Page]**
>
> "Honorable members of the jury, good morning.
> 
> Today, Cameroon is experiencing a significant digital shift. With mobile internet penetration growing every year, almost every sector is moving online. The real estate market is no exception. Physical 'House for Rent' signs on streets are being replaced by digital listings on web portals and social media.
> 
> However, this rapid digital expansion has created a paradox: **we have more listings than ever, but less clarity**. The lack of centralization and validation has led to what we call 'information chaos'—duplicate postings, outdated offers, and complete opacity in pricing history. The digital platforms exist, but they operate in silos.
> 
> Our project, **CentralImmo**, was born out of this digital gap. It is our response to the question: *How can we leverage technology to bring structure, order, and trust to this growing market?*
> 
> On behalf of our team—Azebaze Bahehebeg, Mangouo Jacky, and myself, Azefack Kelcy—we are honored to present our work. As the orchestrator, I will guide you through our slides in English, while my colleagues will explain the technical implementations in French. Let us take a look at our roadmap."

---

### Option 3: The Trust & Transparency Hook ("Rebuilding Market Trust")
*Best for: Emphasizing the business, economic, and security value of the platform.*

> **[Slide 01 — Title Page]**
>
> "Distinguished members of the jury, good morning.
> 
> If there is one word that defines any successful transaction in real estate, it is **Trust**. Yet, in Cameroon's current property landscape, trust is a rare commodity. With over 80% of intermediaries operating informally, consumers suffer from severe information asymmetry. 
> 
> Without centralized data, it is impossible for a buyer or renter to know the true value of a property, or to track how prices are changing over time. Redundant posts waste time, and unverified listings expose users to scams.
> 
> We believe that the key to restoring trust is **data integrity**. By building a system that automatically collects, intelligently deduplicates, and historically tracks real estate listings, we can empower consumers and bring transparency to the Cameroonian market.
> 
> This is what we have designed and built with **CentralImmo**. 
> 
> Joining me today are my colleagues, Azebaze Bahehebeg and Mangouo Jacky. I will run the narrative of this presentation in English, and they will elaborate on our technical choices in French. Let us walk you through our plan."

## 🗺️ How to Transition to the Plan and Introduction

Once you finish your opening hook on **Slide 01**, you must transition smoothly to **Slide 02 (Plan)**, and then hand over the presentation as you enter the **Introduction (Slide 03)**.

### Step 1: Transitioning to the Plan (Slide 02)
*(Move to Slide 02)*
> "To guide you through our work, we have structured our presentation into five main parts:
> 1. **First**, we will explore the core **Introduction** and problem statement.
> 2. **Second**, we will present our **Host Company**, Smart Service Hub, where this project was developed.
> 3. **Third**, we will cover the core of our **Technical Work**, including architecture, scrapers, and our Deduplication Algorithm.
> 4. **Fourth**, we will review our **Results and Assessment**, showing the system in action.
> 5. **And finally**, we will conclude with our **Perspectives** for future development."

### Step 2: Transitioning to the Introduction (Slide 03)
*(Move to Slide 03 — Contexte et Problématique)*
> "Let us begin with our first part: the **Introduction**. As I mentioned during my opening remarks, the Cameroonian real estate market is expanding but suffers from deep fragmentation. 
> 
> To understand how this fragmentation affects daily operations and why it requires a data-driven solution, let me hand over the floor to my colleague, **Azebaze**, who will detail the problem statement and our research question in French. 
> 
> *Azebaze, s'il te plaît, tu as la parole.*"

## 💡 Delivery Tips for Kelcy

1. **Stance & Body Language:** Stand tall, keep your shoulders back, and look directly at the jury. Do not look at the projector screen or your notes while delivering the opening hook. Use open hand gestures.
2. **Pacing & Pauses:** Do not rush. Let the hook sink in. When you describe the apartment price confusion (e.g., *"150,000 CFA... 180,000 CFA... 200,000 CFA"*), pause briefly between each number to emphasize the chaos.
3. **Voice Modulation:** Start with a warm, welcoming tone for the greetings. Shift to a slightly more serious, engaging narrative tone during the hook, and return to a clear, professional, structured tone for the Plan.
4. **Trinôme Coordination:** When you introduce Azebaze and Jacky, briefly gesture toward them with an open palm. They should smile/nod politely to the jury when their names are called.
